    01 · Shield

    Close every backdoor.

    Most home routers arrive with a remote-management channel for your provider, a legacy shell and a guessable setup PIN. Bastion lists each one in plain words and closes it from your desk.

    Remote channels
    TR-069 / CWMP, TR-369 / USP, UPnP IGD
    Legacy services
    Telnet, SSH on default port, WPS PIN
    DNS
    DNS-over-HTTPS or DNS-over-TLS, your resolver
    Privacy
    _nomap network names, private Wi-Fi address check
    Router access
    Local only. Bastion talks to the router inside your network.
    Gateway 192.168.1.1

    02 · Boost

    Faster Wi-Fi. Same router.

    In an apartment building your router fights a dozen neighbours for the same slice of air. Bastion surveys the spectrum, picks the quietest channel and moves your network there.

    Bands
    2.4 GHz · 5 GHz · 6 GHz
    Channel survey
    Every 10 minutes, in the background
    Example result
    296 → 412 Mbps on channel 149 (+39%)
    Dead zones
    Signal map from a two-minute walk around your home
    Hardware
    None. Works with the router you have.
    5 GHz spectrum · live surveyCH 36 → 149
